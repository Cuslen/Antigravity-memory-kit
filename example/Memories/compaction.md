# 2026-10-24: Antigravity compaction

## Fact
Antigravity compacts the chat automatically at roughly 243,000–265,000 tokens, without warning. Only what is in a file survives in detail.

## Why
Twice a project decision existed only in the conversation and was gone after the compaction.

## How to apply
1. Save decisions, preferences and corrections immediately (checkpoint before every answer).
2. After a compaction, read INDEX.md again and work from the files, not from the summary.
3. To look something up in the old conversation, use read_transcript.py (`last`, `search`, `images`).
